The margin carries the signal
A light ground has one real weakness: a thin rule over white captured content is easy to miss. So the mark on the sheet stays quiet — principle 1 says a good capture must look like one image — and findability moves to the margin, where the ground is always paper and contrast is guaranteed no matter what was captured.
confident — 1 px, nearly invisible
flagged — 1.5 px + numbered margin marker
gap — dashed, always labelled with what was lost
The marker's number is the same number the repair queue uses.
