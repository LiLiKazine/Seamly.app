Seamly

Capture beyond the screen.

Seamly turns a scroll into a single, seamless long screenshot — of any app. Everything happens on your device.

Getting started

There are three ways to make a long screenshot, and they all end up in the same place.

  • Record. Tap Record on the home screen and choose Seamly in the system broadcast sheet. Switch to the app you want and scroll steadily. Stop the broadcast from the red indicator or Control Centre, then return to Seamly — your capture is waiting.
  • From Video. Already have a screen recording? Seamly reads it with the same frame-picking code the live path uses.
  • From Photos. Pick a set of overlapping screenshots you took by hand. Seamly recovers the scroll order from the pixels, even if you pick them out of order.

Scroll steadily

Seamly banks a frame each time you have scrolled far enough to need one. If you scroll faster than it can keep up, you will feel a short buzz — ease off and carry on. A capture with a genuine gap will say so rather than hide it.

If a join looks wrong

A bad stitch is not a dead end. Seamly enumerates its own weak points and asks about them one at a time, zoomed to the spot. Most flagged joins turn out fine, so the affirmative answer is the wide one. When a join really is off, drag the two halves until they line up, or use Adjust manually for exact numbers.

Exporting

Save to Photos, share as a PNG, copy to the clipboard, or export a PDF. Very long captures are paginated in the PDF.

Requirements

iOS 26 or later. Seamly needs permission to add photos to your library only when you save a capture there.
