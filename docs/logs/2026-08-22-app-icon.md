# 2026-08-22 — The app icon: "Ruled, three uneven"

The shipped icon was still the pre-Paper spike artefact: a blue→teal gradient
behind rounded, shadowed, stacked cards with dotted separators. It contradicted
every foundation in `design-system/tokens/colors.css` — warm `#ece9e3` ground,
square corners, depth from rules rather than shadow, one restrained accent — and
`design-system/readme.md` is explicit that no palette was ever sampled from it.

## What shipped

A full-bleed ink field with three horizontal joins at **unequal** spacing, the
middle one carrying `--mark-flag`: the uncertain seam. The unequal gaps are what
stop it reading as a barcode, and the off-centre accent is what gives the mark a
top and a bottom.

| Element | Y | Height | @1024 | Fill |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Field | 0 | 100% | 0 → 1024 | `--icon-field` `#1f1d1a` |
| Join 1 | 26% | 2.8% | 266, h 29 | `--icon-join` `#ece9e3` |
| Join 2 | 47% | 2.8% | 481, h 29 | `--mark-flag` `#8a6219` |
| Join 3 | 73% | 2.8% | 747, h 29 | `--icon-join` `#ece9e3` |

Percentages are authoritative. `scripts/make-app-icon.swift` asserts the derived
pixel table equals `[266, 481, 747]` and `h 29`, so editing the constants without
updating the docs fails the run rather than drifting silently.

## Why the icon is not a picture of what the app does

Five rounds tried to draw the process — overlapping captures becoming one long
image — and every one collapsed into a glyph that is already taken: copy, menu,
download, hamburger, waveform, perforation, barcode, spool, gunsight. A
three-stage verb does not survive compression into one static mark. The rejected
directions and their reasons are on the **How the icon was chosen** card
(`design-system/guidelines/app-icon.card.html`).

Two failures worth keeping, because they are not obvious:

- **Ink on paper, never paper on paper.** The first round drew a white `--sheet`
  capture on `--paper`, which is **1.1:1**. Those tokens were contrast-solved for
  type on a ground, not a shape on one.
- **Metaphors invert semantically.** A run of dashes through a gap is a
  perforation, and perforations mean *tear here*. Wide gaps mean *separated*.
  Both are the opposite of a seamless join.

## `--icon-field` / `--icon-join` are theme-stable on purpose

The spec is that dark keeps the same field and the same paper joins and changes
**only** the accent. `--ink` and `--paper` both flip under `[data-theme="dark"]`
(`#f2efe9` / `#1a1815`), so building the mark on them would invert it at night —
the joins would become cuts in a sheet, reversing the material argument the mark
rests on.

So `tokens/colors.css` defines `--icon-field: #1f1d1a` and `--icon-join: #ece9e3`
in `:root` **only**, deliberately absent from the dark block, exactly as `--sheet`
is fixed white in both themes. The accent needs no handling: `--mark-flag`
already lifts to `#d9a544` in its dark scope. **Do not "tidy" these into
`--ink`/`--paper`.**

## No tinted asset

`Contents.json` previously declared three appearances. It now declares two, and
`icon_tinted.png` is deleted: iOS derives the tinted appearance itself, and what
carries there is the value structure — dark field, light joins. Providing a
hand-made grayscale would be a third thing to keep in sync for no gain.

## Verification

Not "it builds", because a build proves nothing visual here:

- **Pixels asserted, not eyeballed.** Every join's first and last row, the field
  row either side of it, and exact token RGB — plus a check that all 1024 rows are
  a single flat colour, confirming no antialiasing softened the rules.
- **Rendered and looked at**, per the repo's own rule, at 1024 and down the
  ladder 180/120/87/80/60/58/40/29 with a squircle mask and LANCZOS downscaling.
  One correction to that test: `29` in a pixel ladder is **29 px**, the @1x
  notification size that no modern device renders. 29 **pt** rasterises to 58 px
  @2x and 87 px @3x, both of which hold cleanly. At a literal 29 px the joins are
  under a pixel each and the rhythm does compress.
- **Compiled catalog inspected.** `assetutil` on the built `Assets.car` confirms
  two renditions — `AppIcon` default → `icon_light.png`, `UIAppearanceDark` →
  `icon_dark.png`. A build can succeed while an appearance is silently dropped.
- **Installed and launched on the iPhone 17 simulator**, and the springboard
  screenshotted: the mark reads at home-screen size and is distinct from every
  neighbour.

## The generator is committed

`scripts/make-app-icon.swift` **parses** the hexes out of
`design-system/tokens/colors.css` rather than hardcoding them, and fails loudly
if a token is missing or is not 6-digit hex. The icon therefore stays derivable
from the design system: change a token, re-run, done. Core Graphics only — no new
dependency, consistent with first-party-frameworks-only.

Antialiasing is switched **off**. Every edge already lands on a whole pixel, so
antialiasing could only soften the rules — the same reason the SVG source
specifies `shape-rendering="crispEdges"`.

## Also on the design system side

`AppIcon` is a real component (`design-system/src/marks/AppIcon.jsx`) with a
`.d.ts`, a `.prompt.md` carrying the geometry table and the do-not-invert rule,
and an `@dsCard` under **Marks** showing light, dark, the unmasked source and the
full size ladder. It renders from tokens, so the card and the shipped PNGs cannot
disagree about colour.
